# Data

Two folders. `raw/` is every probe run, cleaned. `crosslink/` links the reconstructed Charter to those runs and
holds data only. Both are regenerated by the scripts in `../reconstruction/` (see its README).

## raw/

Only the transcripts: `runs/<run>.jsonl`, one row per sample, all models, all 29 runs (1,404 samples).

Row fields: `sid` (`<run>/<model>|<prompt_id>|<rep>`, unique), `run`, `study` (`eval-awareness` or `charter`),
`model`, `prompt_id`, `rep`, `group`, `pair`, `effort`, `provider`, `messages` (exactly what was sent),
`reasoning` (the CoT), `content` (the answer), `finish`, `prompt_tokens`, `completion_tokens`,
`reasoning_tokens`, `cost`. Cleaning: one schema across runs, reruns of the same key keep the last row,
text stripped. Logprobs, judge labels and prompt files are not kept (each row's `messages` is the full prompt). The originals stay in `../out/`.

## crosslink/

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `document.json` | the reconstruction in reading order: `items` (headings `H`, notes `N`, gaps `G`, passages `P`, variants `V`), `excluded` (recurring text judged not to be Charter), `params` |
| `links.json` | one row per (target, sample) where the sample reproduces the target |
| `samples.json` | metadata of the 884 Muse Spark Open charter-study samples that were searched |

A passage carries `pid` (stable id: hash of the text it was first stored with), `text`, optional `label`,
`grown` (true when the text was extended to where its unprompted samples keep agreeing, see `grow.py`), `verdict` (`level`: `word`, `gist`,
`nondiscriminative`, `superseded`; `summary`), `tests` (`probe`, `run`, `result`), and `support`:
`unprompted`, `near`, `echo` sample counts, `runs_unprompted`, and `tier` (`solid` = 3+ unprompted samples
from 2+ runs, `weak` = 1+, `none`).

A link row: `target` (passage `pid`, or `h<index>` for a heading), `sid`, `coverage` (share of the target's
6-word sequences found in the sample), `prompt6` / `prompt3` (share of the target's 6- / 3-word sequences
present in that sample's own prompt), `cls`, and `spans`: character ranges of the match in the sample's
`reasoning` and `content`, plus, for near and echo links, in `prompt` (the user messages joined with `\n`).

Words are matched lowercased, without punctuation, with I/me/it folded onto Muse and my onto its (the CoT flips
between "I must", "it must" and "Muse must"), and without the hedges samples add or drop (materially, reasonably,
generally).

`cls` is what decides whether a sample counts:
- `unprompted`: `prompt6` = 0 and `prompt3` < 0.35. The model produced the text. Only these count.
- `near`: the prompt shared some 6-word sequence, or at least 35% of the 3-word ones (a partial cue or paraphrase).
- `echo`: the prompt contained at least half the passage (verification and multiple-choice probes).
